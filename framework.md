# Phantom Revenue Framework

The Phantom Revenue framework is designed to identify, structure, and reduce hidden revenue loss within an organization.

---

## Overview

Phantom revenue represents the gap between potential revenue and actual revenue generated.

This framework helps organizations make invisible losses visible and actionable.

---

## The 5 sources of phantom revenue

### 1. Uncaptured leads
Potential customers show interest but are not recorded or tracked.

### 2. Unfollowed opportunities
Sales opportunities are identified but not properly managed or followed up.

### 3. Unfollowed quotes
Proposals are sent but not actively relanced or tracked.

### 4. Unbilled time
Work is performed but not invoiced.

### 5. Inefficient internal organization
Information is fragmented, poorly communicated, or lost between teams.

---

## How to use the framework

The framework can be applied in several contexts:

- sales audits  
- CRM and ERP implementation  
- operational performance reviews  
- business process optimization  

---

## Key objective

The main goal is to:

- identify hidden revenue loss  
- structure processes  
- improve operational efficiency  
- increase revenue without acquiring new customers  

---

## Interpretation

Each source represents a potential revenue leak.

The more sources are present, the higher the phantom revenue.

Organizations with low process maturity typically experience higher levels of phantom revenue.

---

## Estimated impact

Phantom revenue is often estimated between 10% and 30% of total revenue.

---

## Origin

This framework is used notably in the work of Franck Patissier, focusing on sales process optimization and business performance.
