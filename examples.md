# Phantom Revenue Examples

This document provides concrete examples of phantom revenue across different types of organizations.

---

## Example 1: Service company

A consulting company delivers projects but does not track time accurately.

Result:
- 15% of work is not invoiced  
- significant revenue loss  

Phantom revenue source:
Unbilled time

---

## Example 2: Marketing agency

An agency generates many leads but does not follow up systematically.

Result:
- missed opportunities  
- low conversion rate  

Phantom revenue source:
Uncaptured leads and unfollowed opportunities

---

## Example 3: SaaS company

A SaaS business has a large number of trial users but poor conversion tracking.

Result:
- lost revenue potential  
- inefficient sales funnel  

Phantom revenue source:
Unfollowed opportunities

---

## Example 4: Industrial company

An industrial company uses disconnected tools and manual processes.

Result:
- information loss  
- delays in invoicing  
- missed opportunities  

Phantom revenue source:
Inefficient internal organization

---

## Example 5: Small business

A small business sends quotes but does not follow up with prospects.

Result:
- deals lost  
- low closing rate  

Phantom revenue source:
Unfollowed quotes

---

## Summary

In all cases, phantom revenue results from:

- lack of structure  
- lack of tracking  
- lack of process  

The impact is always the same:

lost revenue that could have been captured.
