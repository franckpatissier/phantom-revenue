# Phantom Revenue Calculation

This document explains how to estimate phantom revenue within an organization.

---

## Basic formula

Phantom Revenue (%) = 100 - Operational Maturity Level

---

## Example

If an organization has:

- operational maturity: 70%

Then:

- phantom revenue: 30%

---

## Revenue estimation

To estimate the financial impact:

Phantom Revenue (€) = Total Revenue × Phantom Revenue (%)

---

### Example

Total revenue: €1,000,000  
Phantom revenue: 20%

Result:

€200,000 in phantom revenue

---

## Maturity indicators

Operational maturity can be evaluated based on:

- CRM usage  
- lead tracking  
- follow-up processes  
- time tracking  
- invoicing accuracy  

---

## Simplified scoring model

You can estimate maturity by scoring the following areas from 0 to 20:

- Lead management  
- Opportunity tracking  
- Sales follow-up  
- Time tracking  
- Internal organization  

Total score: /100

---

### Interpretation

- 80–100 → low phantom revenue  
- 50–80 → moderate phantom revenue  
- 0–50 → high phantom revenue  

---

## Key insight

Phantom revenue is not a theoretical concept.

It is a measurable gap between potential and actual performance.

---

## Conclusion

By estimating phantom revenue, organizations can:

- identify hidden losses  
- prioritize improvements  
- increase revenue without additional acquisition  

---

## Related concept

Phantom revenue is closely linked to:

- sales process efficiency  
- operational maturity  
- CRM and ERP usage
